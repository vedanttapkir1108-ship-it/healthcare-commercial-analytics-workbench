# Executive summary

## Evidence
IQVIA public research indicates oncology spending is projected to rise from $252bn in 2024 to $441bn in 2029, an implied CAGR of 11.8%. Four therapy areas—oncology, immunology, diabetes and obesity—represent 43% of global pharmaceutical value in the cited 2025 publication.

## Proposed profitable workflow
A commercial analytics workbench can prioritize brands, indications, geographies and HCP segments for deeper analysis, while exposing source lineage, quality checks, assumptions and model limitations. This targets the business tension IQVIA itself describes: convert increasingly large healthcare datasets into fast, differentiated, trustworthy insights while protecting privacy and governance.

## Value hypothesis (not a realized IQVIA result)
If a client improved commercial prioritization by only 1% of the projected oncology increase, the addressable value signal would be approximately $1.89bn. This is a scenario for discussion—not a claim of savings or revenue. A client would need IQVIA data and an approved test design to validate it.
